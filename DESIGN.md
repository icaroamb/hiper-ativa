## Brand & Style

This design system defines the digital flagship for an elite medical aesthetics clinic led by female healthcare specialists in Anápolis, Goiás. The visual expression moves decisively away from commercial clinical sterility, loud neon hues, kitschy beauty clichés, and faux-modernist glassmorphism. Instead, it champions quiet luxury: an architectural, high-end editorial atmosphere rooted in poise, anatomical precision, and understated feminine empowerment.

The aesthetic blends high-fashion editorial layout principles with the clinical rigor of advanced medicine. Key characteristics include:
- **Quiet Luxury & Restraint:** Generous spatial composition, breathing room, and deliberate stillness.
- **Architectural Editorial:** Strong contrast between classical Didone letterforms and technical modern grotesque body typography.
- **Materiality & Subtlety:** Hairline dividers referencing precious metals, warm parchment-like backdrops, and graphite accents rather than harsh digital grays.

## Colors

The palette balances medical authority with warm skin undertones and precious metallic warmth:

- **Graphite (`#222222`):** The primary dominant color for editorial headlines, high-contrast borders, and primary brand markers. Replaces flat charcoal with a nuanced stone-mineral depth.
- **Rich Black (`#000000`):** Reserved for ultimate focal points, crisp display iconography, and deep contrasting moments.
- **Warm Parchment (`#faf6f4`):** The default canvas background. Warm, tactile, and soothing, it counters hospital white without drifting into muddy beige.
- **Rose Gold (`#b17c75`):** Secondary accent used for delicate hairline rules, monogram accents, active indicator lines, and metadata.
- **Deep Rose Accent (`#906054`):** Tertiary action color engineered specifically for high-priority CTA buttons and critical interactive triggers, paired exclusively with crisp white (`#ffffff`) text to ensure uncompromising contrast and legibility.
- **Nude Rosé (`#f2c9c3`):** Subdued surface tint for soft selection states, badge fills, and delicate container highlights.
- **Logo Gray (`#cccccc`):** Used strictly for muted monogram underlays and passive architectural grid alignments.
- **Muted Text (`#7a7a7a`):** Calibrated for secondary medical captions, procedure metadata, and supporting descriptions.

## Typography

The typography pairs Didone couture aesthetic with functional clinical clarity:

- **Bodoni Moda:** Drives the editorial prestige of titles, headlines, and luxury treatment names. Its sharp vertical stress and delicate hairlines evoke bespoke medical artistry.
- **Work Sans:** Serves as the technical grounding system for diagnostic descriptions, treatment plans, clinical parameters, navigation, and body copy.
- **Uppercase Tracking:** All category headers, step indicators, and clinical tags leverage `label-caps` set in uppercase Work Sans with wide tracking (`0.14em`) to maintain an architectural, curated tempo.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an editorial 12-column grid system (4 columns on mobile, 8 on tablet, 12 on desktop) punctuated by deliberate whitespace.

- **Generous Canvas Margins:** Desktop layouts embrace wide side margins (`margin-desktop: 4rem`) and expanded element separation (`space-2xl: 5rem`) to create an unhurried, boutique atmosphere.
- **Hairline Structural Rules:** Sections are defined not by colored container boxes, but by 1px solid hairline dividers in rose gold (`#b17c75`, opacity 0.35 to 0.6) or soft graphite tint (`#222222` at 0.08).
- **Asymmetrical Editorial Staggering:** High-profile doctor portraits and clinic spaces alternate between full-column image placements and generous offset text blocks with ample breathing room.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic drop shadows, floating bubbles, and blurred translucent surfaces. Depth is purely tactile and physical:

- **Hairline Framing:** Depth is established through 1px crisp boundaries using `#b17c75` at 30% opacity or `#222222` at 10% opacity.
- **Surface Layering:** Elevated surfaces sit on a micro-tinted off-white background (`#ffffff` on `#faf6f4`), creating clear physical planar separation without artificial projection.
- **Ambient Contact Shadow (Modals only):** Deep, diffused ground shadow reserved exclusively for clinical booking dialogs: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(34, 32, 30, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design language uses sharp geometry (`roundedness: 0`), reinforcing surgical accuracy, architectural discipline, and high-fashion luxury.

- **Corners:** Buttons, consultation cards, modal dialogs, and text fields feature 0px border-radius.
- **Purity of Line:** The clean, unrounded edges echo the structural lines of the brand typography and provide high contrast against the organic curves of the human body and portraiture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Consultation / Booking):** Background `#906054`, text `#ffffff` (`label-caps`), padding `1rem 2.25rem`, border `1px solid #906054`. Hover transitions to `#222222` background and border.
- **Secondary / Editorial:** Transparent background, `1px solid #222222` border, `#222222` text. Hover fills to `#222222` with `#ffffff` text.
- **Tertiary (Text with Line):** Transparent background with no container. Work Sans uppercase with a 1px `#b17c75` underline offset by 6px.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Single bottom hairline rule (`1px solid #b17c75` at 50% opacity) or full rectangular crisp box (`1px solid #e0d8d4`) over `#faf6f4` background.
- Floating label in `label-caps` (`#7a7a7a`). On focus, border sharpens to `#222222` with zero glow.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp 0px borders with `#faf6f4` canvas and micro-hairline dividers.
- Hover interaction triggers a subtle 1px border shift to `#b17c75` and an inner image zoom transition.

### Treatment Chips & Filter Tags
- Flat rectangular geometry, `0.5rem 1rem` padding.
- Inactive: `#faf6f4` background, `1px solid #e2dad6`, `#7a7a7a` text.
- Active: `#222222` background, `#faf6f4` text, no rounded corners.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sharp square boxes (16x16px) with 1px `#222222` borders. Active state displays a solid `#906054` inner square.

### Doctor & Protocol Bio Cards
- Full-bleed monochrome or desaturated portrait photography framed by thin rose gold dividers. Medical credentials, CRM registration numbers, and specializations are set in uppercase tracked Work Sans.
